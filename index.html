Faces

A map of faces, as an AI model imagines them. Each portrait was asked for the same way: a neutral man or woman from that country. The result is not a truth, but more a mirror held up to the model. Move across the globe and the features shift, sometimes subtly, sometimes abruptly. Some change is real, some is just stereotypes.

Population is treated only loosely. Countries with more inhabitants are given more unique faces; smaller ones, fewer. A way for the crowded places to feel crowded, and the sparse ones emptier.
